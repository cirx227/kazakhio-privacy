Kazakh.io: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026 · App: Kazakh.io (Android package com.aid.kazakhio)

This policy explains what information the mobile game Kazakh.io ("the app") handles, published by Aid gaming ("we", "us"). We keep it short because the app itself collects very little.

1. Information the app stores on your device

To save your progress, the app stores the following only on your device: your settings (music volume, vibration, language), your gold-coin balance, the animal skins you own and have equipped, your ability charges, and the time you last watched a reward ad (for the daily limit). This information is never sent to us and we have no server that receives it. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app chat. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it permanently.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising IDAdvertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from IP address)Advertising, fraud prevention
App interactions with ads (views, taps)Advertising, analytics
Diagnostics and performance data (e.g. crash logs)Analytics, keeping the ad service working

Data is encrypted in transit. How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad Privacy Options. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads). Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rating "PG").

3. Permissions

Internet and network state (to load ads), vibration (optional haptic feedback, can be turned off in the game's settings) and the advertising ID permission used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or files.

4. Purchases

The app has no real-money purchases. Gold coins are earned only by playing or by watching optional reward ads and have no monetary value.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

6. Data sharing, retention and deletion

We do not sell or share any personal information, because we do not receive any. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled and retained under Google's policies linked above. Data stored on your device remains until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the app changes. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
